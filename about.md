My experience spans software development, cybersecurity, DevOps, and machine learning—giving me a broad perspective that I consider a strength. I enjoy thinking holistically about how technology, security, and scalability converge.</p>

These academic foundations have been instrumental in shaping my practical work across diverse roles.</p>

I’m driven by a desire to bridge the gap between cutting-edge innovation and secure, large scale production implementation.</p>

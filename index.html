Hi, I’m Jayshree, and welcome to my website—which is currently a work in progress.

I’m an engineer at heart and an explorer by nature, and that pretty much sums up my life. Whether it’s the career path I’ve curated for myself, how I approach everyday life, or the things I do in my free time—I explore! I also really enjoy solving problems. I approach things with logic, analyze the situation, and then use the tools at my disposal to build solutions.

My experience spans data science, software development, cybersecurity, cloud, and DevOps. As for hobbies, I’m currently exploring Pilates and cycling. Last summer, I was into running and completed my first-ever half marathon. Who knows—one day I might become a Pilates instructor or even run a triathlon! I also love hiking; being in nature grounds and calms me. I love traveling and my dream destinations at this time are (like most people) Japan and New Zealand.

On top of all this, I strongly believe in giving back to the community. Back in Nepal, I was an active organizer and volunteer for various organizations that empowered women in STEM. I took a short pause when I moved to Finland to pursue my Master’s, but now I’m slowly finding my way back to community work.

My vision for this space is to turn it into a collection of my projects, my work, and my thoughts—shared through a blog. I hope this becomes a space where I can showcase both the personal and professional sides of who I am.

I'm active on GitHub and LinkedIn. Feel free to connect!
